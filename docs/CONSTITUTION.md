# Elenchus — Constitution

Read this before writing copy, code, or features. If a request conflicts with this file, stop and ask.

## One sentence

Elenchus exists to bring questioning back to the center of thinking.

## What the name means

The name is the method: Socratic elenchus — short, disciplined questions that test a claim against its own premises until a contradiction, a gap, or a hidden assumption appears.

## Where we start

We do not start from a doctrine to defend.
We start from a statement someone already believes — including statements produced by institutions, media, peers, and machines — and we ask what would have to be true for that statement to hold.

The first question is cheap. The work begins with the second.

## Mission

Restore critical thinking as a practice, not a slogan.

Critical thinking is not:

- a personality
- a political camp
- a pile of correct opinions
- a culture-war costume
- an anti-AI stance
- a debate-club sport

Critical thinking is the habit of refusing to stop at the first fluent answer.

## The drill

1. Define the terms.
2. Name the source.
3. Surface the assumption.
4. Ask what would falsify it.
5. Only then decide.

## Destination

The aim is not to make people clever in argument or cynical about everything.
The aim is to make them harder to fool — including by themselves — so they can act with clearer judgment.

Questioning is the drill. Judgment is the destination.

## v1 product boundary

v1 of the site is a manifesto + method. It collects nothing: no waitlist, no forms, no email capture.
It is not an LMS, not a course marketplace, not a social network, not a news feed, and not a chatbot that answers questions for the user.

If a feature broadcasts answers instead of training the second question, it is out of scope.

## Hard no

- "Unlock your potential"
- "AI-powered learning platform"
- "Revolutionize education"
- Gotcha / dunk / debate-bro tone
- Culture-war revival framing
- Luddite / anti-machine framing (machines produce claims; we examine claims)
- Testimonial carousels, fake urgency, growth-hack CTAs
- Adding pages, auth, or pricing without an explicit human decision
